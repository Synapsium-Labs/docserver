- **Deviations.**
  - D-8950 (accepted at the review): `DocFrame.setReading` carries the mode to a later remount as a replay of the latest reading mode over the new mount's own port at its next `ready` (after the queued prefs and appearance, before `onReady`), not in the seed and not serialized (the plan says it "seeds a later remount"). Why: bootstrap bytes (87 left). The limits, stated in the README: it is not a paint guarantee (before that ready the new frame has not heard the mode, and no initial paint or race was measured here); and a mount that omits a piece of chrome (`features.chrome`) does not build it, so `'off'` cannot restore it in place. The consumer's first-paint and exit and restore proof belong to the consumer's own wave; this library prescribes nothing about disabling restorable chrome. Proof: `R8` in `test/browser/frame-reader.test.ts` (a real remount after `setReading`, the new frame in the mode once ready, `'off'` restoring the chrome it built in place, and a mount without a rail having none to restore), and the replay order (prefs, appearance, reading) pinned in `test/host-mount.test.ts`.
  - D-8951 (accepted at the review): the text size's `m` is the reader's existing typography for each face and device, not a flat 18 px (the plan names 16, 18, 20 and 22 px). Measured computed body sizes at `m`: serif 18 px, serif at a phone width (700 px and under) 17 px, sans 17.5 px and sans at a phone width 16.5 px; `s`, `l` and `xl` are 16, 20 and 22 px in every face and width. On a sans phone `s` and `m` differ by only 0.5 px (against 3.5 px from `m` to `l`): that is deliberate, and it is no reason to change the default or add a control. Why: `m` keeps today's look byte for byte. Every cell is asserted in `test/browser/reader-size.test.ts` (Z1), with an absent and an unknown stored size reading as `m`; the README and CHANGELOG say so.

## Review send-back round (reader-mode fixes)

- **F1, hidden overlays.** Entering reader mode left an open contents drawer logically open while its chrome was hidden: every press was taken for a press on an overlay, so the top band sent no `menu`, taps did not turn and Escape closed an invisible drawer. The reader now closes the drawer through its owner (`overlays.close('drawer')`) at every mode change, before the attribute moves, and the `contents` command (default rail and the Classic and Editorial rail) and the `i` key open nothing while a mode is on (rows R49 to R51). Left alone, because the mode leaves them visible: the full-screen figure, the quick jump, the keys sheet, the settings menu, the preview card and the "Changes since" menu. The last was first taken for hidden: its picker is in the visible review strip (not in the bar tools), so the first fix round's automatic close of it at a mode change was wrong and was removed at the second review; the menu stays open across entry, screens to scroll and exit, and Escape and its trigger dismiss it (case in `test/enhance-reader.test.ts`, `S2` in `test/browser/reader-startup.test.ts`). Proof for the drawer: jsdom cases in `test/enhance-reader.test.ts` and `R7` in `test/browser/frame-reader.test.ts` (390 px, default layout: the top band sends `menu`, a tap turns the screen in `screens` and does not in `scroll`, Escape sends `exit`).
- **F2, start-up order.** A mode asked for while `enhance()` is starting (a remount made from the host's own callback, for example) was applied before the features were built, so the reader found no rail class to take off and the rail added `has-toc` afterwards: invisible rail padding in the text column at 1240 px and wider. It is now kept until the features are built (a host callback that runs during the build included: the newest valid mode wins, and a value that is not one of the three replaces nothing) and applied after the feature loop. Proof: jsdom cases with a rail-building document (start-up, a callback during the build, an invalid value) and `S1` in `test/browser/reader-startup.test.ts` (1400 px: the column of a root entered later, and `'off'` giving the rail and its padding back with the same elements). The frame path (which waits for `ready`) and older hosts are unchanged.
- **Release notes.** The CHANGELOG now names the one layout snapshot that moved (the settings menu's DOM, 20 lines for the Text size fieldset) and counts 19 bridge cases (9 `reading` and 1 `reader` sent by the parent, 8 `reader` and 1 `reading` sent by the frame). Published commit messages are not rewritten.

## Results: the hosted runs, and the ownership record

- **The first required CI run was red.** WebKit `T4` failed on both legs (`back turn 4: row 0` 12.28 px below the bottom edge, against the 1.5 px rule). The cause is the snap of spec 5.3 (a back turn whose new top lies in the text above the table snaps that line whole, lowering the row by the snapped part), not an engine difference; the geometry assertion was corrected (test only, `pages.ts` unchanged): `rowBelowEdge` judges a new top inside the table by the old rule and a new top in the text above it by the snap, and `T4b` reaches that case on purpose. The old rule was reproduced red in Chromium at 12.953125 px before the change; the snap guards are `P86` to `P88` in `lx6-mutations/task-2.md`. The WebKit explanation is inferred from Chromium; only the hosted runs measured WebKit.
- **One unrelated snapshot failure, once.** On one leg the Chromium `layout-corpus` case `rail/malformed-marks` lost the active state of the first Contents entry; it did not recur on the later run. Local evidence: 200 of 200 isolated runs and 10 of 10 whole-file runs passed, 60 of 60 in four parallel loops. With fonts already cached, the same separate-call snapshot lacked the state in 29 of 40 runs; with `createPages` removed in place 31 of 40, and with the reader also stubbed 27 of 40, so the rate does not depend on this change. The attribution is qualified: a plausible pre-existing timing gap in the layout-corpus runner (the scroll spy's first activation runs in the start relayout, one frame after `enhance`, and `runCase` snapshots without the neutral-rail waiter that `rail/plan-marks` has); it was not measured on the hosted run, and the runner was not changed here.
- **Ownership gaps, recorded as gaps.** The settings menu's layout snapshot (`conformance/layout/settings/menu.dom.txt`) was edited outside the path claim the first wave held. The CI-fix commit and the commit that named the layout move as a trailer were made after that claim lapsed at its hard cap. A later claim that covers every path does not backdate either.


## Second review round (the corrections, as measured)

- **The "Changes since" picker is visible.** It sits in the review strip, in the text, not in the bar tools the reader hides. The first fix round's closing of its menu at a mode change was wrong and is gone: only the contents drawer is closed by the reader at a mode change (and it cannot open while a mode is on). Cases: the picker case in `test/enhance-reader.test.ts` and `S2` in `test/browser/reader-startup.test.ts` (picker shown with a mode on, menu open across screens, scroll and exit, Escape and the trigger dismiss it), red against the old close (row R52). Mutation row R43 and R44 of `lx6-mutations/task-1.md` are historical: R43's edit no longer exists, and R44's case was removed with the premise that nothing else could open a hidden overlay, which was false for the changes menu only because it is not hidden.
- **Start-up requests.** A mode asked for while `enhance()` is starting is kept until the features are built, host callbacks during the build included, and the newest valid mode wins; an invalid value replaces nothing (rows R45b, R46b, R53, R54). The remount evidence did not measure a concurrent-update browser race (a `setReading` made while an `update()` is in flight): the cases pin the queue, the order and the latest-wins rule, and the README says so. The consumer's own first-paint and exit and restore proof remain its own wave's.
- **Text size and print.** Medium stays as ruled (18, 17, 17.5 and 16.5 px); Small, Large, XL and the stored fallback are unchanged. The claim that print keeps 11 pt whatever the size was true of serif only. Measured by T14p in Chromium (1400 px, print media): for each face the printed body size, h2 size and column width are the same for all four sizes; serif prints at 14.6667 px (11 pt) and sans at 17.5 px (its screen rule is outside any media query: pre-existing, unchanged, and no print baseline is introduced here). The comments that said `m` is a flat 18 px (`Prefs.size`, the stylesheet comment, the size test header) are corrected.
- **Mutation records.** Rows are dated by the revision they were measured on (24be393's tree for R42 to R48, f1f3ab1's for R49 to R51, 5242485 for the second-round rows and the re-measured R42, R45b, R46b, R49 to R51 and C7), and the records say which are historical.
- **Old-cap.** The old-cap gate runs only on a manual dispatch and was skipped in the hosted runs of this pull request. Both old-cap legs remain owed on the release candidate; nothing here claims them.
- **Kept as recorded:** T4 and T4b stay separate from the unrelated rail snapshot failure; the 1.1.0 and 1.0.0 hash lines are the published ones; the settings menu snapshot is the one layout move; the two ownership gaps stand as gaps.
