# C61: Task 18 may correct two physical-input premises in `test/browser/rail-interaction.test.ts`, without accepting rail behavior

*Recorded 2026-10-21, coordinator.* · [Decision log](README.md)

## Decision

This is a [C54](C54.md) shared-test exception for those two cases only, not a CSS, source or performance decision.
- The phone scrim case derives a point from the actual drawer and viewport rectangles, proves that the point lies outside the drawer and inside the scrim, and verifies the browser's hit target before a real mouse click. The former fixed x coordinate lies inside the accepted wider drawer. Preserve connected-opener focus return, drawer closure, Tab cycling, disconnected fallback, Escape and teardown assertions; a synthetic click or forced locator event is not a scrim-hit test.
- The desktop outside-link case uses enough real rendered headings to exceed the actual Contents panel and proves that overflow and the target's outside position before scrolling the document. Keep real scroll spy, h2/h3 active state, panel movement and containment, and the existing 1 px window-scroll bound. Do not mock the spy, inject fixed layout heights, weaken the outside precondition or force the panel to move with test code after the document scroll.
- Reverse each corrected physical input independently and require its intended behavioral assertion to fail, then restore and pass. These are test-input corrections, not evidence that the unchanged production behavior passes. Report any newly reached production failure separately; this grants no `rail.ts`, stylesheet, RF3, geometry, golden, scanner, harness, configuration or other shared-test change, and no Task 18 or group acceptance.

## Why

The accepted drawer geometry invalidates the fixed click coordinate, and the desktop fixture must establish the overflow its assertion claims.

## Cost if wrong

A fixture repair could conceal incorrect focus or scrolling, so real hit testing, overflow and the original behavioral assertions remain required.
