# C82: The click guard prevents a link's activation whatever the button

*Recorded 2026-10-26, coordinator.* · [Decision log](README.md)

## Decision

WebKit follows a link in place on an `auxclick` of any button but the right one, a back or forward button included. The prototype's guard prevented only the forwarded buttons, so on WebKit synthetic auxclicks of buttons 2 to 4 navigated the sandboxed frame away from its document. Two instrumented CI runs established it: the link lookup came back empty after those auxclicks, while the frame still had its one load and its links after a real right click. The guard now prevents every link activation and forwards only a click or the middle button's auxclick; the "P6 other buttons" row asserts that nothing crosses and the frame keeps its one load and its links, on both engines. WebKit cannot start locally (its system libraries are missing and installing them needs root), so CI is its only witness. With the fix, run `40368219321` at `4b4dc6f3f` passes on Node 22 and 24: 3,259 tests in 116 files on both engines, and the packed-consumer smoke (82 of 82 cases).

## Why

"no link ever navigates the frame" is a frame invariant, and a back-button press over a link is ordinary reading.

## Cost if wrong

A host loses a middle-button behaviour it expected; the forwarded set is unchanged.
