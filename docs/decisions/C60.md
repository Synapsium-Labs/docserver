# C60: Fold reading counts use pre-feature source, and stored folds are validated before controls are created

*Recorded 2026-10-21, operator.* · [Decision log](README.md)

## Decision

These settle two callback-visible choices in the diagnosed fold correction; they do not accept an implementation.
- Capture the source identities and reading contributions after initial storage and pending-preference setup, before feature chrome is added. Text edits or new author blocks inserted by later startup callbacks do not enter that frozen reading-count or source cohort. This is a deliberate earlier cutoff, not a claim that callbacks cannot edit the document or that every byline and fold measurement is atomic.
- Read stored folds and validate the held heading/member structure before creating fold controls, notes or hidden state. A synchronous stored-fold getter therefore no longer sees already-created controls or notes. A heading removed by that getter must not receive new controls afterward.
- Keep the existing pre-getter membership cutoff: no second scan, neighbor absorption or reassignment after that getter. Discard stale held associations rather than transferring their totals into another section. The existing prebuild empty/false/no-effect Folds facade remains unchanged; no reservations or half-ready facade follow from these choices.
- The compact integer source-count and attention-owned completion design still needs its final bounded contract review, exact writer scope and integrated input. This records product behavior only: it does not grant a lifecycle edit or a new internal export, waive independent model or whole-task gates, relax geometry, RF3, CSP or corpus limits, or authorize a source writer to overlap the current table owner.

## Why

Source-only fold times and safe startup must have explicit semantics when synchronous host callbacks edit the document.

## Cost if wrong

Later startup text edits are excluded from fold counts, and the stored-fold getter observes fewer library controls than before. The operator chose both bounded tradeoffs explicitly.
