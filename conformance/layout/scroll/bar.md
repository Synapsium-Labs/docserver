# Reading a long page

**Note** · 2026-10-20 · Status: Draft

A page opens with its title and a few paragraphs before its first section, so nothing is being read yet when it
opens: the running head holds the title, and the bar holds the tools the layout builds.

The bar sits at the top of the reader and stays there while the page scrolls by. A thin line under it shows how far
the reader has come, and the running head names the section being read once the title has scrolled away.

On a narrow screen the bar slides away while the reader scrolls down and comes back when they scroll up, so the
text gets the whole screen while it is being read.
