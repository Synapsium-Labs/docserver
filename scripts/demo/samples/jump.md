# Example guide

**Guide** · 2026-10-20 · Status: Draft · **Owner:** project-a team

## In short

This example guide has many sections, so the quick jump has something to find. Every name in it is a stand-in.

## 1. Getting started

A reader opens a page and wants one part of it. The quick jump lists the sections, and typing narrows the list.

### Opening the jump

The slash key opens it, and so does the button in the bar.

### Choosing a row

The arrow keys move the selection, and Enter goes there.

## 2. Reading a page

A page leads with its title and a short summary, then its numbered sections.

### Headings and numbers

The numbers hang in the margin, and the jump shows them before each title.

### Long sections

A long section folds, and the jump opens the fold it goes into.

## 3. Moving around

The keys sheet lists every key that works on the page, with the colour key below it.

### The keys sheet

The question mark key opens it, and Escape closes it.

### Going back

Escape closes the jump and gives the focus back to where it was.

## 4. Open questions

Two things are still open, and each can be answered in a line.

1. Should the jump remember the last query?
2. Should a figure show its number in the list?
